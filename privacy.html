Inquiry form

When you submit the form on our homepage, your name, email, company and message are sent by email to our inbox so we can reply. The website does not keep a copy of form entries in a database. To limit spam, the server briefly records a hashed form of your IP address for about ten minutes.

Information you send

If you email us, we use the information to review your inquiry, reply, and discuss potential services. Do not send passwords, API keys, client records, sensitive personal information, or confidential files through the inquiry form.

Website hosting

The hosting provider may process routine connection information, such as IP addresses, requests, and security logs, to deliver and protect the site. This static website does not embed analytics trackers or third-party advertising scripts.
