around AI.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Built for the
real world.

What is an AI Business Harness?+

It's the control system around an AI model. The model can interpret information and suggest what to do. The harness connects it to your business tools, controls what it's allowed to access, manages each step of the workflow, and records what happened. It reduces the risk of unsupported answers and incorrect actions through validation, permissions, and human review.

Do we have to replace our current software?+

Usually not. We start with the applications your team already uses and evaluate supported integrations before considering browser or desktop automation.

Can an AI agent send messages on its own?+

Only when a workflow has been specifically tested and authorized. Our default approach is to prepare drafts and route customer-facing communication for human approval.

Will you need access to every account?+

No. Each workflow gets the minimum access it requires, with separate client environments and clearly documented boundaries.

What if our software doesn't have an API?+

In some cases a controlled browser or desktop workflow can help. We evaluate reliability, permissions, and recovery requirements before recommending it.

How do we know whether it's worth it?+

We start with a baseline and choose metrics such as time saved, follow-up completion, response delays, exceptions, or qualified opportunities. We don't promise results before measurement.
